Introduction

About Me

Hi, I'm Sharayu Pravin Rathod, an Assistant Professor in the Department of Pharmaceutics at GES's Sir Dr. M. S. Gosavi College of Pharmaceutical Education and Research, Nashik, with over four years of teaching and academic experience. Currently pursuing my Ph.D. in Pharmaceutics, my core research focuses on nanotechnology, novel drug delivery systems, and skin-targeted topical formulations

Alongside my academic responsibilities, I am actively engaged in scientific writing and institutional quality-assurance initiatives, contributing to over 11 publications in peer-reviewed journals and multiple book chapters. I also serve across various administrative capacities, including NAAC Criterion VI committee work, digital outreach, and laboratory coordination.

Deeply committed to student mentorship and academic excellence, I have guided numerous undergraduate students in research presentations, competitive events, and projects that have received recognition at university-level competitions like Avishkar. I look forward to driving innovation in pharmaceutical sciences through dedicated research and effective pedagogy
